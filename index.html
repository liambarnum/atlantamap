          Download & share

          Importing GeoJSON, GPX or KML adds its points as pins. If the file contains lines, you will be offered the option to replace the corridor with them — that is how you load the official BeltLine alignment.

          Basemap

          Stored in this browser only (localStorage), never sent anywhere but Google. Needs the Maps JavaScript API enabled on a project with billing on.

          About this data

          The corridor and access points bundled here are hand-traced approximations, accurate to roughly a block. They are fine for planning a walk and wrong for anything that needs real precision. Import the official alignment to replace them.

          Your pins and route stay in this browser. The one exception is Find a place: pressing enter sends that search text to OpenStreetMap's geocoder, or to Google if the Google basemap is running. Typing alone never leaves the page.
