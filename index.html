          Importing GeoJSON, GPX or KML adds its points as pins. If the file contains lines, you will be offered the option to replace the corridor with them — that is how you load the official BeltLine alignment.

          Basemap

          Stored in this browser only (localStorage), never sent anywhere but Google. Needs the Maps JavaScript API enabled on a project with billing on.

          About this data

          The trail comes from OpenStreetMap, so the line is real geometry rather than a sketch. Gaps in the loop are real too — where the map shows a break, the trail is not built.

          Access point names are hand-curated and their positions snapped onto the trail; a few in the north were poor guesses before snapping and are worth checking.

          Your pins, route and favourites stay in this browser. Two things leave it, both only when you ask: the text you type into Find a place once you press enter, and the names sent by Find these on the map. Both go to OpenStreetMap's geocoder, or to Google's if the Google basemap is running.
